What is Ikura?

Back to the homepage

Ikura is a fast and simple code highlighter designed to spice up your language designs.
Language design is cool and all, but to demo your syntax, you and your audience are forced to parse through plain white text
—unless you go through the effort of making a full VS code extension or something similar,
which requires quite a lot of work.

Ikura makes it easier to demonstrate your language. After providing some keywords and optionally choosing colors,
you receive a beautiful demonstration of your syntax.
