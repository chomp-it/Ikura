Ikura

Ikura is a fast and simple code highlighter designed to spice up your language designs.
The thought is that language design is cool and all, but you and your audience are forced to parse through plain white text
--unless you go through the effort of making a full VS code extension or something similar.

Keywords

For example:
function, async, and let in JavaScript;
unless, begin, and rescue in Ruby;
switch, echo, and while in PHP.





Literals

For example:
integers like 96 or 2004
floats like 06.081995
strings like "Nemo my name forevermore"
Or any other primitives.


You cannot change the literals that are recognized for now



Conjunctions

For example:
do in Ruby,
-> in Erlang or CoffeeScript,
"in" in OCaml
